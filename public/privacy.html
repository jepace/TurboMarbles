Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

TurboMarbles is a free online marble racing game run by an individual hobbyist at marbles.pacehouse.com. This page says what we keep about you and why. Short version: only what the game needs, never sold, never used for ads.

What we keep

  • Your account: your player name; your password, stored only as a salted hash (we can't read it); and, if you give one, your email address.
  • If you sign in with Google: Google's ID for your account (so we recognise you next time), and your email address if Google has confirmed it. We get your name once, to suggest a player name. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.
  • If you use a passkey: its public key and a label for the device. The private key never leaves your device.
  • Sign-in sessions: a random key kept in your browser; we store only a hash of it, so we can sign you out of a device.
  • Your games: the moves, results, ratings and statistics of games you play while signed in, and messages you send to friends or in a game's chat.
  • Technical: your IP address, briefly, to limit abuse (too many sign-in attempts), and server error logs.

What we use it for

Running the game: signing you in, your stats, rating, friends and messages, game analysis, and keeping the site safe. Email is used only to confirm your address and to reset your password.

Who sees it

Other players see your player name, rating, results and games you played with them. Nobody else gets your data, except services the site needs to run: our email sender (Resend) receives your address to deliver account emails, and Google handles "Sign in with Google". Robot league games are public.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser's local storage for your sign-in key and your settings (board look, sound). No advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can change your name, email and sign-in methods, and delete your account, in Settings. Deleting your account removes your account, email, sign-in methods and messages; past games stay in other players' histories as "deleted player".

Children

TurboMarbles is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will say so, with a new date. Questions: use the game's report button, or the contact shown on the site.
